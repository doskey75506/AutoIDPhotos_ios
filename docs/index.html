Help & Support

Your photo, ready to print.

Create ID photos on your iPhone. Adjust dimensions, position the head, choose a background, and export a single photo or a print sheet.

Photo processing runs on your device. No account is required.

Four steps

  1. Confirm photo dimensions and background.
  2. Take or choose a clear, front-facing photo.
  3. Align crown and chin until the box turns green.
  4. Tap Generate, then Share to save or export.

Common questions

Why is the head box red?

After capture or import, the box checks the crop against your current dimensions. Drag inside to move it and drag the corners to resize it. Keep the crown, chin and sides of the head inside. If the crop needs more space, move the box, adjust the dimensions or choose a photo with more room.

A green box confirms framing only. Check the generated edges and background yourself. Head boxes are not detected on the live camera stream.

Can the crop extend above my original photo?

White, light grey, blue and red backgrounds can fill missing space above the source. Original-background mode requires the entire crop inside the photo. Missing space at the sides or bottom is still rejected.

What do the background colours affect?

They change the photo background, not the sheet separators. Light grey is RGB(240,240,240), blue is RGB(67,142,219), and red is RGB(255,0,0). Print separators stay medium grey. The original-photo preview keeps its original background; generate a new result after changing settings.

How do I get the sharpest output?

Keep Original Resolution is enabled by default. It retains pixels in the head-based crop. Turn it off to choose a fixed DPI. A print JPEG is limited to 20 megapixels; share the PDF for a sheet retaining the single photo's detail. More output pixels cannot restore detail missing from the source.

How do I save or share?

Use the share button under the single photo or print sheet. Choose Save to Photos or Share JPEG; the sheet also offers Share PDF. If Photos access is denied, use Open Settings to review the app's permission. Limited access also supports saving.

How should I print the sheet?

Use the configured paper size and 100% actual-size printing. Disable Fit to Page, Fill and borderless enlargement. Prefer PDF and measure one printed photo to confirm its physical dimensions.

How do I change language or camera?

Use the globe button for English or Simplified Chinese. The camera starts with the front lens; use the camera-switch icon for the rear lens. System pickers and permission prompts use iOS language settings.

  2. 拍摄或选择清晰正面照。

常见问题

方框为什么变红？

拍摄或导入后，方框按当前规格检查裁切范围。拖动框内移动，拖动四角缩放，确保头顶、下巴和头部左右边缘都在框内。空间不足时，可移动方框、修改规格或更换留白更多的原图。

绿框仅表示取景范围合适，仍需检查生成后的边缘与背景；实时视频中不进行头框检测。

裁切可以超出原图顶部吗？

纯白、浅灰、蓝色、红色背景可补齐原图上方空白。原始背景要求整个裁切区域都在原图内；左右或底部空间不足仍会被拒绝。

背景颜色会改变打印分界线吗？

不会。设置改变照片背景；浅灰为 RGB(240,240,240)，蓝色为 RGB(67,142,219)，红色为 RGB(255,0,0)。打印分界线保持中灰色。原图预览保留原始背景，更改设置后需重新生成。

如何保留最大精度？

默认开启“保留原图分辨率”，保留根据头部大小计算出的裁切区域像素。关闭后可指定 DPI。打印 JPEG 上限为 2000 万像素，需要保留单张精度的拼版请分享 PDF；增加像素不能恢复原图缺失的细节。

如何保存和分享？

点击单张或打印拼版下的分享按钮，可保存到照片或分享 JPEG；拼版另可分享 PDF。照片权限被拒绝时，点“打开设置”检查授权；有限访问也可保存。

如何保证打印尺寸？

选择设定的纸张，使用 100% 实际大小打印，关闭适应页面、填满和无边距放大。优先使用 PDF，打印后用尺核对单张尺寸。

如何切换语言和摄像头？

地球按钮切换英文与简体中文。默认使用前置摄像头，可通过切换图标改用后置摄像头；系统选择器与权限弹窗使用 iOS 的语言设置。
