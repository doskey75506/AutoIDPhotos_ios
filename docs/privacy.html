Privacy

Your photos stay in your workflow.

This policy covers the iOS app ID Photo Maker, bundle identifier com.jialishen.AutoIDPhoto.

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Developer: Mobile Soft. Contact: shenok.smth@gmail.com.

Photos and face processing

The app processes photos you choose or capture, finds head position and generates a foreground mask to replace the background. These operations run on your device. Photos, face-position coordinates and masks are not sent to the developer or an external image-processing service.

The app uses head geometry for framing. It does not identify who you are, create an identity profile, or use your photos to train a model. The current app has no account system, advertising SDK or developer analytics SDK.

Permissions

  • Camera: used for the live preview and photos you take. The app stops its capture session when it leaves the capture flow or goes into the background.
  • Photos: the system picker lets you choose source photos. Saving or checking library access requests Photos read/write permission. You can choose limited or full access where iOS offers those options. The app does not scan your library.

Review or revoke access in iOS Settings for this app. Camera and library permissions are requested when you use the relevant features.

Storage, retention and deletion

  • Working photos, generated previews and head positioning are held in app memory. They are replaced during use and are not deliberately saved as an editing project.
  • Language and photo settings are saved on the device until changed or the app's data is deleted.
  • Sharing creates JPEG or PDF files in the app's temporary directory. The app does not currently delete each exported file immediately after sharing; files can remain until iOS clears them or you delete the app and its data.
  • Photos or files you explicitly save remain in Photos, Files or another chosen destination. Delete those copies in that destination, including Recently Deleted where applicable.

Deleting the app and its data removes its local settings and remaining app-container files; offloading the app can retain app data. This does not remove copies saved elsewhere or copies in backups. Review your iCloud, backup and receiving-app settings separately.

Sharing and system services

Using the share sheet sends the selected output to the app, person, storage service or printer you choose. Those destinations apply their own privacy practices. Photos saved to your library may sync through iCloud Photos if you have enabled it. Operating-system diagnostics and backups depend on your Apple settings.

Image processing is local; this statement does not imply that user-initiated sharing or system-managed cloud storage stays offline.

This support website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, forms or third-party fonts. If published on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles website requests and may process technical request information under its Privacy Statement. This is separate from processing photos inside the app.

Contact and changes

If you email Mobile Soft at shenok.smth@gmail.com, your address, message and any attachments are received through Gmail for handling your support request. Correspondence may remain in the support mailbox; you can request its deletion by email. Email-provider handling is described in Google’s Privacy Policy. This support correspondence is separate from photos processed locally inside the app.

Use the support contact for privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to support. Do not include personal photos unless necessary. If processing changes, this policy will be updated and its date revised.
