Version 1.1 update — build-specific information
The developer has confirmed that version 1.0 is live. This supplement is for the version 1.1 update.

This build updates camera orientation handling to use the active camera's rotation coordinator. Preview and photo capture use their respective rotation angles, and switching cameras binds the coordinator to the new camera.

The initial interface language is Simplified Chinese when Chinese is the device's primary preferred language, and English otherwise. A saved manual language selection takes precedence. Features are otherwise the same across regions.

The app now opens directly to camera capture. After taking or importing a portrait, tap Generate Photos to open the settings and quick guide. Choose a document-size reference or Manual Settings; named custom presets can be saved and reused. Presets are crop references, not a guarantee of acceptance or digital-upload compliance, and include official source links.

On initial face detection, the head box expands upwards by 20% and downwards by 10% of its detected height, clipped to the image. Later manual edits are not expanded again.

To check these changes:
1. Launch the app and check that camera capture appears immediately.
2. Check portrait orientation, switch cameras, take a photo and check the resulting image.
3. Drag the labelled face box, then tap Generate Photos.
4. Select a photo preset; numeric dimensions appear only under Manual Settings.
5. Save a named custom preset, reopen the sheet and select it again.
6. Tap Confirm & Generate to create the ID photo and print sheet, then test saving and sharing.
7. When framing is invalid, use Apply Settings and Adjust Framing, correct the box and try again.
8. Use the globe button to switch languages and check that the choice is remembered after relaunch.

White is the default background; Restore Defaults resets the settings draft without deleting saved custom presets. The gap input and explanation are hidden, while the minimum-gap layout rule remains active. Document and background menu options that cannot fit the current source are disabled. Expand Unavailable Options for specific framing/retake guidance, or return directly to camera capture. Solid backgrounds still permit top padding outside the original photo.
