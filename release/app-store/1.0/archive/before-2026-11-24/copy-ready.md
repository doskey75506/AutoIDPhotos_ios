### Description

```text
Create ID photos and arrange them on a print sheet, with control over the portrait, dimensions and background.

CAPTURE OR CHOOSE A PHOTO
Use the built-in live camera preview or select an image from your photo library. The camera starts with the front lens, with a button to switch between front and rear cameras.

DETECT AND ADJUST
After capture or import, automatic face detection sets a head box. Drag the box to move it or drag a corner to resize it. Check the crown, chin and head position before generating your photo.

CHOOSE THE BACKGROUND
Keep the original background or use portrait cutout to replace it with white or light grey. Check the hair, shoulders and other edges in the preview.

CONTROL THE DIMENSIONS
Set the photo width and height, crown-to-chin height range, paper size, DPI and spacing. The default layout places eight 35 × 45 mm photos on a 6 × 4 inch sheet. Generate a new sheet after changing the settings.

EXPORT YOUR RESULTS
Export a single ID photo as a JPEG, save the print sheet to Photos, or share the sheet as JPEG and PDF. Grey spacing makes individual photo boundaries easier to see when cutting.

ENGLISH AND SIMPLIFIED CHINESE
English is the default interface. Switch languages using the globe button; your choice is remembered.

Image processing takes place on your device. Photos are not uploaded for processing.

For printing, select the matching paper size, use 100% / actual size and turn off automatic page fitting or enlargement. Check the final photo dimensions with a ruler.
```

### Description

```text
制作单张证件照与多张打印拼版，自行调整人像位置、照片尺寸和背景。

拍照或从相册选图
在页面内查看实时相机画面并拍摄，默认使用前置摄像头，也可切换后置；或直接选择相册中的照片。

自动定位，直接微调
拍摄或导入后自动检测人脸并设置头部方框。拖动框内移动位置，拖动四角调整大小，核对头顶、下巴和人像位置后再生成。

人像抠图与背景处理
保留原始背景，或通过人像抠图换成白色、浅灰色背景。生成后可预览并检查发丝、肩膀等边缘。

自定义照片与打印参数
调整照片宽高、头顶至下巴高度范围、打印纸大小、DPI 和照片间距。默认将 8 张 35 × 45 毫米证件照排在 6 × 4 英寸纸张上，更改参数后，可按新设置生成拼版。

单张与整版分别导出
单张证件照可导出为 JPEG；打印拼版可保存到相册，或分享为 JPEG、PDF 文件。照片之间使用灰色间隔，便于辨认每张照片的裁切边界。

中英文随时切换
界面默认英文，点击地球按钮即可切换简体中文，语言选择会保留。

图像处理在设备上完成，不为处理上传照片。

打印时请使用对应纸张与 100% 实际大小，关闭适应页面或自动放大，并用尺核对成品尺寸。
```
