### Description

```text
Create single ID photos and multi-photo print sheets, with control over dimensions, head position and background.

START WITH YOUR SETTINGS
Each launch begins with specification settings and a short four-step guide. Confirm photo size, head-height range, background and paper size before opening the camera. Settings are remembered. English is the default interface; switch to Simplified Chinese using the globe button.

CAPTURE OR CHOOSE A PHOTO
Use the live camera preview, starting with the front camera, or choose an image from your photo library. Switch between front and rear cameras when taking a photo.

DETECT AND ADJUST
After capture or import, face detection sets a head box. Move the box or resize its corners to align the crown, chin and head position. As you adjust the box, green indicates that the crop fits the current settings; red explains a framing problem. This checks crop space, not background quality or acceptance of the finished photo.

CHOOSE YOUR BACKGROUND
Keep the original background or use portrait cutout with white, light grey, blue or red. With a replacement background, missing space above the source image can be filled in the selected colour; the head, sides and bottom must still fit. Check hair and shoulder edges in the generated preview.

CONTROL SIZE AND RESOLUTION
Adjust photo dimensions, head-height range, paper size and spacing. Defaults arrange eight 35 × 45 mm photos on a 6 × 4 inch sheet. Generate a new sheet after changing settings.

By default, the single photo keeps the source crop's pixel dimensions. Alternatively, choose a fixed DPI. JPEG files include DPI metadata for the selected physical size. Print-sheet JPEGs are limited to 20 megapixels; the PDF uses the single photo's full generated resolution without that sheet limit. Preview captions show the JPEG pixel dimensions.

SAVE OR SHARE
Export a single JPEG or a print sheet as JPEG or PDF. Each photo's share menu offers Save to Photos and photo-access controls, with a shortcut to system settings when permission needs attention. Grey spacing separates photos; cutting marks appear when space allows.

Image processing takes place on your device. Photos are not uploaded for processing.

Requires iOS 17 or later. For printing, use matching paper, 100% / actual size and no automatic page fitting or enlargement. Prefer PDF and check the printed dimensions with a ruler.
```

### Description

```text
制作单张证件照与多张打印拼版，自行调整照片尺寸、人像位置、背景与打印规格。

先确认规格，再开始拍摄
每次启动先显示规格设置与四步简要说明。确认证件照尺寸、头高范围、背景及纸张大小后，再进入拍摄页。参数会保存在本机。界面默认英文，可通过地球按钮切换简体中文，并记住语言选择。

拍照或从相册选图
在页面内查看实时相机画面并拍摄，默认使用前置摄像头，也可切换后置；或直接选择相册中的照片。

自动定位，拖框微调
拍摄或导入后自动检测人脸并设置头部方框。拖动框内移动位置，拖动四角调整大小，使方框对齐头顶、下巴和头部。调整时实时检查裁切范围：绿色表示符合当前取景规格，红色会提示空间不足等原因。此检查仅判断裁切空间，不代表抠图质量或成品已通过审核。

保留原始背景，或换成纯色
可选原始背景、白色、浅灰色、蓝色和红色。选择替换背景时，裁切超出原图顶部的空间可用相同颜色补齐；头部、左右及底部仍需完整。生成后请检查发丝、肩膀等边缘。

自定尺寸与输出精度
调整照片宽高、头顶至下巴高度范围、纸张大小及照片间距。默认将 8 张 35 × 45 毫米照片排在 6 × 4 英寸纸张上。更改参数后，可按新设置生成拼版。

默认保留原图裁切区域的像素，也可关闭此选项并指定 DPI。JPEG 写入与设定物理尺寸相符的 DPI 信息。打印 JPEG 上限为 2000 万像素；PDF 使用生成的单张照片排版，保留单张精度，不受该整版像素上限影响。预览下方显示 JPEG 的实际像素尺寸。

保存单张，或导出整版
单张证件照可导出为 JPEG；打印拼版可导出为 JPEG、PDF。两种照片各自的分享菜单均提供“保存到照片”和照片访问权限操作，需要调整权限时可快捷打开系统设置。照片之间使用灰色间隔，空间足够时附带外部裁切标记。

图像处理在设备上完成，不为处理上传照片。

需要 iOS 17 或以上。打印时使用对应纸张与 100% 实际大小，关闭适应页面或自动放大。建议优先使用 PDF，并用尺核对成品尺寸。
```
